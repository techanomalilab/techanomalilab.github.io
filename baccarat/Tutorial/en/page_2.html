Bet Mechanism

  • Choose Your Bet: Place chips on the betting field to select your wager.
  • Single-Ring Bets: Function like standard roulette bets, with adjusted payouts.
  • Double-Ring Bets: Parlay bets requiring both rings to win simultaneously.

Game Elements

  • Outer Ring & Inner Ring: Two separate number wheels spinning independently.
  • Ball: Lands in a slot that determines the outcome for both rings.
  • Single Ring Bets: Traditional-style roulette bets placed on one wheel only.
  • Double Ring Bets (Parlay): Special bets requiring both rings to hit the chosen outcome.
  • Chips: Represent bet values.
  • Result Display: Shows winning numbers for both rings and total payout.
